What a DMesh session costs

## Building one: reconnect churn at a fixed load

| reconnect period | sessions/s | Mrps | ARM us/req | p99 us |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| never | 0.0 | 0.010304 | 38.084 | 1221.0 |
| 4000 | 2.5 | 0.010080 | 38.282 | 1248.0 |
| 1000 | 10.1 | 0.010132 | 38.400 | 1249.0 |
| 250 | 40.1 | 0.010152 | 38.657 | 1256.0 |
| 60 | 167.5 | 0.010204 | 39.321 | 1248.0 |

DPU cores = 0.389 + 0.0734e-3 x sessions/s
building and tearing down one session costs 73 ARM core-us
which is worth about 2 requests at 38.08 us/req
