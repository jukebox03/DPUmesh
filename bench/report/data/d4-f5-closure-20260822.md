# D4 / F5 closure receipt — 2026-08-22

## Scope

This closes `PLAN.md` D4 and F5. F4, F6, node-density/capacity work and all
performance defects/cost items were deliberately left out of scope.

## Cause

D4 and F5 were two observations of one lifetime break across the host, gRPC
EventEngine and DPU:

1. FIN/reset control descriptors were not an incarnation fence. The host could
   recycle a source port before the DPU had removed the old `(pod, port)`
   session, and the DPU acknowledged a request FIN before both Linkerd output
   halves had retired.
2. A public gRPC `Channel` can disappear long before gRPC releases its internal
   `Endpoint`. Failed handshakes and reconnects destroyed their Endpoint first,
   selected the graceful QP close, and then waited up to five seconds per QP
   for data custody. Those waits were serial on the reactor and accumulated
   during the policy campaign.
3. If DPU teardown ran while its unit pool was dry, the stalled retry consumed
   `lifecycle_pending` before the EOF/control unit was available and could
   leave the refused session key behind.

## Fix

- Track zero-length FIN/reset controls in the host TX FIFO and do not reuse a
  source port until their DPU ACK retires. The DPU now defers a request FIN ACK
  until both directions and all upstream state are gone; reset ACK follows
  removal of the old request key.
- Give each client connection a one-shot lease shared by the public Channel and
  its gRPC Endpoint. Channel/Endpoint abandonment and late successful connects
  use `dmesh_abort_qp`, so failed handshakes cannot queue serial five-second
  graceful closes. Explicit source reset performs the same safe DPU cleanup
  without incrementing the `peer/poison` failure counter.
- Re-arm stalled DPU lifecycle work whenever EOF, peer FIN, backend FIN or ACK
  publication is still under allocation/backpressure.

for native, preload and gRPC (`fail=0` for all three), with no denied,
no-policy or poison counter movement.

none despite 1,029 denied/reconnect opens.

registrations that the generation fence safely aborted, not live residue.
