What a DMesh session costs

## Building one: reconnect churn at a fixed load

| reconnect period | sessions/s | Mrps | ARM us/req | p99 us |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| never | 0.0 | 0.010523 | 51.946 | 1200.0 |
| 4000 | 2.5 | 0.010293 | 53.171 | 1284.0 |
| 1000 | 10.0 | 0.010069 | 55.921 | 1398.0 |
| 250 | 36.1 | 0.009271 | 63.899 | 2716.0 |
| 60 | 109.7 | 0.007338 | 92.455 | 2766.0 |

DPU cores = 0.548 + 1.1997e-3 x sessions/s
building and tearing down one session costs 1200 ARM core-us
which is worth about 23 requests at 51.95 us/req

## Carrying them: live sessions at a fixed outstanding window

| threads | conc | live sessions | Mrps | ARM us/req | p99 us |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 128 | 1 | 0.165447 | 3.812 (+0.0%) | 1228.0 |
| 2 | 64 | 2 | 0.170780 | 6.153 (+61.4%) | 1271.0 |
| 4 | 32 | 4 | 0.176078 | 8.829 (+131.6%) | 1307.0 |
| 8 | 16 | 8 | 0.159492 | 15.539 (+307.6%) | 1343.0 |

The outstanding request window is the same on every row of the second
table, so a rising us/req is what more live sessions cost, not more load.
Every point carried fail=0, drops=0, reorder=0 and worker_fail=0.
